Today I finally [released](https://github.com/gotson/komga/releases/tag/1.26.0) the new version of the WebUI, called NextUI. It's been almost exactly 1 year since I announced the rewrite of the UI. Back then I thought I could get a first version out by year's end, but life took over, and both my spare time and my motivation had their ups and downs.

NextUI is in public beta, running in parallel alongside the existing WebUI:
- Not all features are available yet. You can check [this issue](https://github.com/gotson/komga/issues/2267) for a list of what is available and what is yet to be implemented.
- If you have any feedback/questions, or if you observe any issues, with the NextUI, please [open a discussion](https://github.com/gotson/komga/discussions/new?category=nextui-beta-test) in the dedicated category.
