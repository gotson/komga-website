# Read with Sumi

[Sumi](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6777730252?pt=129005256&ct=komga-docs&mt=8) is a reader for manga, comics and novels on iPhone and iPad. It connects to Komga through the REST API and reads both the comics and the EPUB novels of your libraries. Sumi itself ships no content.

## Install and connect {#install}

You need iOS or iPadOS 15.1 or later and a Komga server that your device can reach.

1. Download Sumi from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6777730252?pt=129005256&ct=komga-docs&mt=8).
2. Open the **Browse** tab and, under **Add a source**, tap **My server**.
3. Under **Komga Server**, enter the server address, such as `https://komga.example.com`, or `192.168.1.10:25600` on your local network. An address typed without a scheme uses https, except on the local network; a public server must use https.
4. Enter your Komga **Email** and **Password**, then tap **Connect**.
5. Your series appear in the **Komga Library**.

The password is kept in the iOS Keychain and is never synced with iCloud.

## Features {#features}

- Paged reading, right-to-left or left-to-right, or vertical scroll; two pages side by side on iPad and in landscape.
- EPUB novels open in a text reader, Japanese vertical text included, and their position is shared with Komga 1.9.0 and later.
- Comic read progress stays on the device, and on your other devices with iCloud sync; it is not sent back to Komga yet.
- Also reads your own CBZ, CBR, ZIP, EPUB and PDF files, opened from Files, AirDrop or Mail.
- Free to read and stream. The Fine Press subscription adds offline downloads, iCloud sync, read aloud and on-device translation for novels, and more themes.
